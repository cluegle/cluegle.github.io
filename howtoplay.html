Cluegle



How to Play

Cluegle is a "daily" guessing game! Each day, you will be shown the Google search results from searching some person, place, thing, event, or idea. The results are redacted, meaning that there are different parts of the results that are covered up. Clicking on these boxes will reveal what's underneath, but at the cost of the number of points indicated. You can track how many points you have in the top right corner. Images and video thumbnails are permanently redacted and cannot be revealed.

Once you think you know what was searched, enter your guess in the search box at the top, but beware! Wrong answers cost a point, and if your point total ever reaches 0, you lose!

Can you figure out the right answer in time? Find out for yourself!
